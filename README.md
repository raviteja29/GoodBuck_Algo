Setting up a Privacy Policy for Meta Business (whether you're setting up a Meta Pixel, running Lead Ads, or using the WhatsApp Business API) is a strict requirement. Meta requires this link during the app or business verification process.

Because a Privacy Policy is a legal document, it needs to reflect your actual data practices. Here is a comprehensive template tailored to meet Meta’s compliance requirements.

Quorq.ai Privacy Policy
Last Updated: 29-06-2026

At [Your Company Name] ("we," "our," or "us"), we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, use our services, or interact with our Meta Business tools (including Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, and WhatsApp).

1. Information We Collect
We collect information that you provide directly to us, as well as information automatically collected when you interact with our platform.

Information You Provide: Name, email address, phone number, and any business details you submit via contact forms or Meta Lead Ads.

Automatically Collected Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and tracking data collected through cookies, web beacons, and the Meta Pixel.

2. How We Use Meta Business Tools
We utilize various Meta Business tools to improve our advertising and user experience. These include:

Meta Pixel & Advanced Matching: We use the Meta Pixel to track user actions on our website. This helps us measure the effectiveness of our advertising, optimize ad delivery, and build targeted audiences for future campaigns.

Meta Lead Ads: If you submit your information through a Meta Lead Ad, that data is securely transferred to our database to fulfill your request (e.g., sending a quote or newsletter).

Meta Custom Audiences: We may use hashed identifiers (like email addresses) to match with Meta's database to show you relevant advertisements.

3. How We Share Your Information
We do not sell your personal data. However, data collected via Meta Business tools is shared with Meta Platforms, Inc. to facilitate advertising services. Meta may use this data in accordance with the Meta Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies
We use cookies to analyze traffic and optimize your experience. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

To opt-out of Meta's interest-based advertising, you can adjust your preferences directly within your Facebook account via the Ad Settings page, or visit the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

5. Data Security & Retention
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. We retain your information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with legal obligations.

6. Your Rights
Depending on your location (e.g., GDPR in Europe, CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email provided below.
